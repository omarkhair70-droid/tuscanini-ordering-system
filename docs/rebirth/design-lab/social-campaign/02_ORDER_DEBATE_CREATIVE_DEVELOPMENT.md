## Brief
Stop making generic product glamour ads. Connect a credible Tuscanini **menu-selection difference** with a human situation; no invented promotions, prices, ingredients, customer behavior, or image/SKU identity.

## STAGE 01 — TRUTH / OPEN HYPOTHESIS
Evidence: Tuscanini's historically supplied real photos (TSC-06 staff with assorted crepes; TSC-08 sandwiches in actual red branded packaging), and a diverse public historical menu covering crepes, sandwiches, pizza and more. Exact product matches and current assortment need merchant validation. The consumer occasion "groups agree to eat but disagree on what" is a creative hypothesis, not a confirmed research result.

## STAGE 02 — TERRITORIES
**A / The Order Debate (selected for one speculative KV):**
Everyone agrees they're hungry; each person's choice differs. Human social tension, real restaurant category variety; question: can this attach to Tuscanini specifically?
**B / The Name Is Not Enough (not selected):**
Actual signature dish names may be difficult to decode without authentic SKU photos; risks repeating prior shallow "what's inside" trope.
**C / The Daily Pause (rejected):**
Generic emotional break; no evidence this is a distinctive Tuscanini occasion.

## STAGE 03 — ART DIRECTION
Split layout, not a food poster. Two opposing statements:
- `كلنا جعانين` — dark agreement.
- `محدش متفق` — vermilion disagreement.
Two **unaltered real-source photographic crops** showing sandwiches in actual red packaging and staff-held crepes, with slightly displaced baselines: visualizes variation in preferences and keeps the restaurant's material world present.
Black and warm white control reading; red is a single decisive voice. No torn-paper texture, AI cheese, drawn food, invented badge, fake logo or price.
Photo filenames from the 12-photo manifest are TSC-08 and TSC-06; the master production crops were made from their rendered, existing website About screenshots because those were the local source images available in the design session. The image file itself is saved as a conversation artifact, **not in this repository**.

## STAGE 05 — FUTURE SYSTEM (NOT EXECUTED)
Recurring territory could vary the conflict: group chat disagreement; "أي حاجة" personality; mixed moods; in-site menu resolution. Each future art asset would require a new genuine photograph and a distinct visual approach, not the same split-photo template.

## STAGE 06 — QUALITY GATES
**Already done:** 4:5 1080 master rendered; typography manually checked and corrected; 360px preview inspected; no fake food/offer/price; self-contained source ZIP exported.
**Not done:** user interviews, blind 3-second brand recognition test, merchant rights/current menu/real logo signoff, paid creative A/B. These require real people and approval. Do not claim conversion uplift or publish from this draft.

### Experiment after merchant approval
Show this campaign and two actual generic competitor category posts to 5–8 people for 3 sec each, random order. Ask unaided brand, understood story, remembered visual and interest in menu. Compare category confusion and brand attribution. The strongest comparison determines whether to keep, revise or retire the direction.
